# Blanks Support — team guide

Our own help desk, replacing Gorgias. Live at
**support.blankssportsnutrition.com**

---

## First-time setup (5 minutes — do this before your first ticket)

1. Go to **support.blankssportsnutrition.com** and sign in with the email
   and password Michael set up for you.
2. Click **Settings** (bottom-left, next to your name).
3. Click **Connect Gmail** and approve. **This step is not optional** — if
   you skip it, your replies will save but never actually send to the
   customer.
4. Set your **signature**: your name as customers should see it (e.g.
   "Melissa Smith", not "Mel") and your title. This is what appears at the
   bottom of every email you send.
5. Send yourself a test: open any ticket, write a reply, send it, and check
   it arrives in your inbox looking right.

## What you're looking at

**The left sidebar** is how you find work:

| View | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Everything that needs someone's attention |
| **My tickets** | Assigned to you — this is your list |
| **Unassigned** | Nobody owns it yet. The triage pile. |
| **All tickets** | Everything, including resolved |
| **Resolved** | Done |

Below that, the same tickets filtered by where they came from — Website
form, Email, and (coming later) Instagram and Messenger.

**The middle** is the conversation. Customer messages on the left, ours on
the right.

**The right panel** is context: who the customer is, their Shopify orders,
their previous tickets with us, plus status, priority, assignment and tags.

## The daily loop

**Melissa runs triage.** She works the **Unassigned** list — answering what
she can, and assigning the rest to whoever should own it.

**Everyone else works My tickets.** When something is assigned to you, you
get an email from hello@blankssportsnutrition.com telling you about it.
Urgent ones say `[URGENT]` in the subject.

**To take a ticket yourself**, open it and click **Claim ticket**.

**To reply**, type in the box at the bottom and hit **Send reply** (or ⌘↵).
It goes out as a real email from *your* address, so the customer sees a
person, not a robot.

**To pass it to someone else**, click their name in the right panel. They
get emailed, and you get moved to the next ticket automatically.

**When it's handled**, hit **Resolve**. If the customer writes back later,
it reopens on its own.

## Two things that trip people up

**"Waiting on customer"** appears after you reply. It means the ball is in
their court — you don't need to chase it, and the system won't nag you
about it. When they answer, it moves back to Open.

**Internal notes are not emails.** The 🔒 Internal note tab is for talking
to each other on a ticket — the customer never sees it. If you're unsure
which mode you're in, look at the composer: notes are tinted yellow.

## Getting chased

If a ticket assigned to you sits unanswered, you'll get a follow-up email:

| Priority | Chased after |
|---|---|
| Urgent | 8 hours |
| High | 24 hours |
| Normal | 48 hours |
| Low | 72 hours |

Each of those emails has **Remind me later** buttons (1h / 4h / 8h / 24h)
if you've seen it but can't act yet. Nothing fires between 9pm and 7am
unless it's Urgent.

## What to report back during testing

We want to know about:

- Anything confusing, ugly, or slower than it should be
- Replies that don't arrive, or arrive looking wrong
- Emails from customers that don't turn into tickets
- Anything you expected to be able to do and couldn't

Send it to Michael. "This felt annoying" is useful feedback — it doesn't
have to be a bug.

## Quick test to run

1. From your personal email, send a message to
   **hello@blankssportsnutrition.com**
2. Watch it appear as a ticket (within a few seconds, no refresh needed)
3. Claim it, reply to it, and check the reply lands in your personal inbox
4. Reply from your personal inbox and confirm it threads back into the same
   ticket rather than creating a new one
5. Resolve it

If any of those five steps doesn't do what it says, that's exactly what we
need to hear about.
